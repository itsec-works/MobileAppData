Talkgrid

Conference schedules on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Talkgrid reads the schedules conferences publish, from a link, a QR code or a file. Star the talks you want to see, keep notes, and get a reminder before they start.

  • A day view and a room grid, with your own filters
  • Stars, notes and reminders that sync through your own iCloud
  • Works offline once a schedule is on your device
  • Now and Next on Apple Watch
  • Reads pretalx, frab, iCal and EMF schedules, or a file you open yourself
