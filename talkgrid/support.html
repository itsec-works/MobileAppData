Support

Questions, problems and ideas.

Get in touch

Open an issue at github.com/itsec-works/talkgrid/issues. Please add your device, your iOS version and the conference you were looking at. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

Add a conference

Tap + and paste a schedule link, scan a QR code, or pick a known server. You can also open a schedule file from Files, Mail or the share sheet.

My conference is not listed

Paste the link to its schedule. To suggest a server for the list, open an issue with its address.

A schedule is out of date

Open the Filter sheet and check that Keep Up to Date is on. With it off, Talkgrid makes no schedule checks and keeps the copy you have.

Stars or notes are missing on another device

Check that both devices use the same iCloud account and that iCloud is on for Talkgrid. Syncing can take a while. A deleted note stays in Settings, My Notes, for 30 days.

Apple Watch

Open a conference on your iPhone first. The watch shows Now and Next for that conference and sets its own reminders for your starred talks.

Talkgrid shows schedules published by conference organizers, and is not affiliated with them.
