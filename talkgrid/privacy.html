Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Talkgrid has no account, no tracking and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

What stays on your devices

The conferences you add, your stars, notes, reminder choices, hidden rooms and tracks, and your watchlist are stored in the app on your device.

iCloud

If iCloud is on, that data and the schedules it needs sync through the private database of your own iCloud account, so your devices stay the same. Apple handles this under its own terms. The developer of Talkgrid cannot read that database.

What the app connects to

  • Schedule servers. Talkgrid downloads schedules from the servers of the conferences you add, and from known conference servers when you search them or use the watchlist. It also loads the logos, header images and speaker photos those schedules point to, usually from the same server, sometimes from an image host the organizer uses. Like any website, those servers can see your IP address and when you connected. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • apps.zwarts.work. When you search known servers, at most once a day, Talkgrid downloads a server list from https://apps.zwarts.work/talkgrid/servers.json. That server and its host can see your IP address when it does.
  • Shared links. Sharing a talk creates a link to https://apps.zwarts.work/talkgrid/talk.html that contains the conference's schedule address and the talk. Talkgrid makes this link on your device without contacting the site. When someone opens the link without Talkgrid, or an app shows a preview of it, their device loads that page, and the site and its host can see their IP address and the link.

Keep Up to Date lets you stop schedule checks for a conference.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are created on your own devices. Your iPhone sends your Apple Watch what it needs directly.

Children

Talkgrid collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting a conference removes it from your devices. Its notes, stars and schedule wait 30 days in case you add it again, then go for good. Deleted notes can be restored in Settings, My Notes. Deleting the app removes what is on that device. You can remove the iCloud data in the Settings app, under your name, iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
